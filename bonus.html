Valiant Alligator and Violet

Meet the Superhero Team

Valiant Alligator is a brave superhero who protects the city from dangerous villains. He has the strength and toughness of an alligator and never backs down from a challenge.

His sidekick, Violet, uses powers inspired by the violet flower. Violet helps Valiant Alligator during battles and uses special abilities to stop their enemies.

Enemies

  • Dark Phantom
    • Major Power: Can become invisible for five minutes.
    • Minor Power: Can make lights flicker.
  • Time Thief
    • Major Power: Can travel two minutes into the past.
    • Minor Power: Can slow down clocks around him.
